Political Attitudes Explorer

This short questionnaire examines three dimensions of political attitudes: economic left–right views, libertarian–authoritarian values, and populist attitudes.

You will be shown 16 statements in a random order. For each statement, select the response that most closely matches your view.

There are no right or wrong answers. Your answers are converted into three anonymous scores and added to the class graph. No name, email address or student ID is collected.
